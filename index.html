HOW IT WORKS

  • STEP 1: TRAIN AI MODEL — Learn patterns from real data distributions
  • STEP 2: GENERATE RECORDS — Create statistically similar synthetic data
  • STEP 3: VALIDATE QUALITY — Ensure fidelity, utility, and diversity
  • STEP 4: PRIVACY CHECK — Run re-identification tests and privacy audits

21

CHALLENGES

  • Rare Events — Difficult to generate accurate synthetic samples of uncommon patterns
  • Complex Relationships — Multi-table relational integrity hard to preserve
  • Temporal Dependencies — Time-series patterns require specialized techniques
  • Computational Cost — Training models on large datasets requires resources
  • Validation Burden — Ensuring quality requires rigorous testing
